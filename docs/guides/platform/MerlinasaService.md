# Merlin as a Service

Clients have asked for a way to take advantage of Merlin without having to partition additional resources, learn OpenShift skills, or acquire new hardware.  

IBM business partners have recognized this market trend and have developed Merlin as a Service.  Merlin as a Service is running on IBM® Power® Virtual Server, IBM’s Power in the cloud offering.  All end users must do is sign into their Merlin environment and get started modernizing and building next-generation applications on IBM i.  The business partners take care of the setup and management of the environment, so no need to worry about getting additional hardware, learning how to install Red Hat OpenShift, or purchasing additional software.

The partners who launched Merlin as a Service are:
- CSI in EMEA
- Sanwa Comtec K.K. in Japan
